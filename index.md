## Capstone Reflection

### 1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?

One thing I found interesting was getting to know more about the HVAC industry and how complicated their product information can be. There are so many different models, serial numbers, and part numbers to keep track of, and some products may have been discontinued or replaced by newer versions while still being used by customers. This makes finding accurate information and providing technical support more challenging than I initially expected. Through our competitor research, we also found that other HVAC companies face similar problems, and some have already developed AI-powered tools to help address them. It was interesting to see how an industry I wasn't very familiar with is starting to adopt AI to solve these practical problems.

### 2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.

I would like to look more closely at how well AI assistants handle multi-step technical support questions. From my experience testing Scout AI, finding information about a specific product was relatively straightforward, but longer conversations could become confusing. We could test a few common scenarios, such as identifying a product with incomplete information or asking follow-up troubleshooting questions, and compare how different tools respond. I think this could help us better understand what makes an AI assistant actually useful.

### 3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?

One thing I would change is to make our competitor testing more systematic. We've been exploring different AI support tools, comparing their features, and looking at user feedback, which has been helpful for understanding what's available. However, I think having a few consistent test cases across different tools would make our comparisons more meaningful. It would also help us distinguish between features that sound useful and those that actually work well in practice.

### 4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?

I would be interested in exploring different ways to combine traditional search methods with LLMs. For example, HVAC model numbers are very specific, so exact or fuzzy matching might work better for identifying products, while semantic search could be more useful for understanding general questions and finding relevant documentation. I think comparing these approaches could be interesting, especially for situations where users provide incomplete information or make mistakes when entering product details.
